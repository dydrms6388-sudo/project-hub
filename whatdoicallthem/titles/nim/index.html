님 -nim

-님 is not really a word so much as a lever. Attach it to a title, a role, a name or a handle, and the whole thing is raised a level.

Hangul님
Revised Romanization-nim
McCune–Reischauer-nim
Literallythe general honorific suffix
Said byany speaker
Said toanyone with a title, a name, or a username

What it actually does

-님 elevates whatever it is attached to. 선생 (teacher) becomes 선생님; 팀장 (team leader) becomes 팀장님; 고객 (customer) becomes 고객님; 부모 (parents) becomes 부모님. In each case the base word is a role, and -님 turns it into a respectful form of address for the person filling it.

It also attaches to names. 김지우 님 or 지우 님 is common in service settings, in writing, and in the growing number of Korean companies that have abolished rank titles and address everyone as name plus -님.

Why it took over online

Korean online communities run almost entirely on 닉네임 + 님 — handle plus -님. It solves the central problem of internet conversation in a hierarchy-conscious language: you do not know the other person's age, gender or position, and asking is intrusive. -님 sidesteps all three.

That makes it the single safest form for a learner joining a Korean game, forum or open chat. Nobody will read it as cold, and staying on it indefinitely is normal.

Where it does not go

-님 is a suffix, not a standalone noun in careful speech. Shouting "님!" happens in games and gets used as internet slang, but in person it sounds abrupt — attach it to something.

It also does not stack with 씨: 지우 씨님 is not a word. Pick one. And in Korean business writing you will see 님 used with the special honorific particle 께 rather than 에게 — 지우 님께 보냈습니다 — which is worth recognising even before you can produce it.

The usual mistake — Using 님 on its own as a shout, and combining it with 씨. Also: assuming it is only for bosses. Customers, parents and strangers all take -님.

Example sentences

김지우 님, 잠시만 기다려 주세요.
Gim Jiu-nim, jamsiman gidaryeo juseyo.
Ms Kim Jiu, please wait a moment.
고객님, 무엇을 도와드릴까요?
Gogaeng-nim, mueoseul dowadeurilkkayo?
How may I help you, sir/madam?
토마토님, 혹시 지금 접속 중이세요?
Tomato-nim, hoksi jigeum jeopsok jungiseyo?
Tomato-nim, are you online right now?
지우 님께 메일 보내 두었습니다.
Jiu-nim-kke meil bonae dueotseumnida.
I have sent an email to Jiu.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Is -nim more polite than -ssi?
Yes, clearly. 씨 marks polite distance between equals; -님 raises the listener above you, which is why it works for bosses, customers and strangers.
Q. Can I use -nim with a first name?
Yes — 지우 님. It is standard in service settings and in companies that have dropped rank titles.
Q. What do I use in an online game?
Handle plus -님. It is the default, and it stays correct however long you play together.
